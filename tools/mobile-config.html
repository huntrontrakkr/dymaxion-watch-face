↗A different view of EarthThe map & the mind behind it

A Dymaxion map unfolds the globe into triangles, bringing the continents together in one connected view. It invites you to see a shared planet, with no single country at its center.

Buckminster Fuller was a designer, inventor and restless thinker, known for geodesic domes and the idea of doing more with less. He developed this way of seeing Earth with cartographer and architect Shoji Sadao. This little watch is our nod to that big imagination.
