↗About the mapBuckminster Fuller’s Dymaxion projection

The Dymaxion map projects the globe onto an icosahedron and unfolds it into flat triangles. The continents stay in one connected piece with little distortion, and no country sits at the center.

Buckminster Fuller, the designer and inventor known for the geodesic dome, published the first version in 1943. He developed the later icosahedral version, which this watch face uses, with Shoji Sadao.
