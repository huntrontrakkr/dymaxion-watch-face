# Dymaxion 0.4.5

The bottom tray can now choose its own page. Set Automatic rotation to Smart
and it shows what matters now: the weather when rain is likely in the next few
hours, the tide as it turns, Health while you are walking, the calendar in the
morning, and otherwise your starting panel. A wrist flick still changes the
page, and the page you chose stays for ten minutes before Smart takes over
again. It works from data the watch already has and adds no sensors.

The night saver can now follow your watch's Quiet Time. Turn on During Quiet
Time in Power & motion and, whenever Quiet Time is on (switched on by hand, by
its schedule, or during a calendar event), Dymaxion treats it as night: the map
reshades every other hour and nothing animates. Use it with or without the
saver's own hours.

Pause in the dark now follows the backlight. At night the face holds its last
picture while the light is off, catches up the moment the light comes on, and
keeps the time current for as long as it stays on.

Times on the map now come in five sizes, from the original small figures up to
large and wide ones, under Map time size in Display. Each label still finds
its own open space on the map.

Tall place times, also in Display, makes the three times beside the clock
larger. It is off by default.

Place icons are now a setting of their own, on by default for every palette.
Each place's map marker appears next to its name in the time zones panel. The
day and night mark beside it is now a small sun or moon, so it can no longer
be mistaken for a marker.

The watch preview on the settings page now matches the watch face exactly, and
the page's wording is simpler and clearer.

Two options are gone. The stacked hours-over-minutes clock is removed; faces
that used it open with the regular clock. The "light the screen, then flick
once" panel gesture is removed too, since it did not work reliably; faces that
used it switch to two flicks.

Fixed: with the Broad or Span clock, the Dymaxion nameplate was partly hidden
behind the clock. It now shows in full.
