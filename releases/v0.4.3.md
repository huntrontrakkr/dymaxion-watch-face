# Dymaxion 0.4.3

A new blue-and-amber faceted globe identifies Dymaxion in the watch's face
selector. It is drawn directly on a 25×25 pixel grid, with a transparent
background and native Pebble colors for crisp edges.

The icon is bundled into the watchface itself and adds no ongoing drawing or
sensor activity. Your layout, palettes and settings stay as you left them.
