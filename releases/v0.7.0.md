# Dymaxion 0.7.0

Draw your own place markers. Choose **Draw your own** as a place's map glyph,
and a 5×5 grid appears, starting from the glyph you had. Tap squares to draw.
Your drawing shows everywhere a built-in glyph does: on the map, in the
time-zone panel, and in the strip between the clock and the map.

Place times beside the clock can now show each place's glyph before its name.
Turn on **Icons beside the clock** in the place-time options; it is off unless
you choose it. Built-in glyphs and your own drawings both appear. Where a row
is tight (a 12-hour time with a day offset), the name shortens first, and a
name cut to a single letter is dropped, leaving the glyph to identify the place.

The face also does less work, with every pixel unchanged. A minute tick
repaints only the clock, the top bar and the bottom panel. When the map is
relit, only the parts near the edge of daylight are read and repainted. The
Sun and Moon markers are placed directly instead of being found by searching
the map. Measured in the emulator, firmware included, a relight minute takes
37% fewer instructions and an ordinary minute 10% fewer: about 18% less
over a day. That is a modest battery gain, since the display and Bluetooth
use most of the watch's power.
