# Dymaxion 0.6.1

The face now does much less work each minute, and it looks exactly the same.

A minute tick repaints only the clock, the top bar and the bottom panel. The
map is left alone unless the Sun has moved, or a marker has.

When the map is relit, only the parts near the edge of daylight are read and
repainted. A typical relight now reads about a sixth of the map data. It does
about a fourteenth of the shading arithmetic.

The Sun and Moon markers are now placed directly, not found by searching the
map.

Every pixel is unchanged. The emulator checks confirm that after a minute tick
or a relight, the face matches a full repaint exactly.
