# Dymaxion 0.4.9

After updating to 0.4.8, the tide chart could go on without its high and low
marks, and its header could show only one of the next high and low, for up
to six hours. The phone kept using tide data saved by the older version,
which did not include every high and low. It now fetches fresh tide data
right away when the saved data is from before the update.
