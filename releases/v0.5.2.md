# Dymaxion 0.5.2

This update reduces CPU work and repeated phone-to-watch transfers while
preserving the face's appearance and its 400 ms minute animation.

- Minute animations reuse their triangle geometry, requiring 57–60% fewer
  application instructions in the measured transitions.
- Routine syncs skip data the watch has already acknowledged. In the default
  stationary-day simulation, messages fell from 144 to 48. Launch and reconnect
  still send the full state, and weather and location checks keep their cadence.
- Weather, humidity and tide updates repaint only the visible bottom panel.
  Unchanged packets and updates to hidden panels avoid unnecessary redraws.
- The step indicator reuses recent Health results, and automatic city naming
  avoids repeated lookups when the location has not changed.

These are measured reductions in CPU work and communication. The improvement
in days per charge still needs to be measured on a physical watch.
