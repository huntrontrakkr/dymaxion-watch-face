# Dymaxion 0.6.0

Take the tides with you. With the Tide panel enabled, **Follow current location**
now selects the nearest NOAA station with hourly predictions as you travel.
It checks about once an hour, within NOAA coverage and 150 km of a station.
Choose any station yourself to keep it fixed instead. Old settings adopt the
automatic mode; select **Keep a fixed station** if you want your previous choice.

The map can now show the Moon at the place on Earth where it is overhead,
shaded for its waxing or waning phase. Enable **Moon on the map** in Map settings;
the existing Moon in the top bar remains independent. **Map rotation** also
offers a 180° view, with place labels and symbols kept upright.

The time-zone strip between the clock and map now offers **Compact strip times**.
It uses the same small pixel numerals as the times beside the clock. Leave it off
to keep the existing larger figures; city labels and day offsets remain readable
with spacing adjusted for either size.

Both map options use the existing cached renderer and power-saving intervals.
Moon positions are calculated on the watch, and tides retain their six-hour
prediction cache unless you move to a different station. No continuous GPS,
per-second animation or extra lunar downloads are required.

The city label already follows your phone about hourly when automatic location
is enabled. The main clock follows Pebble's system time; let the phone/watch
synchronize their time zone after travel. Weather, city lookup and automatic
tides need phone location permission and connectivity.
