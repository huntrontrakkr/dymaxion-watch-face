# Dymaxion 0.4.2

Panel gestures now allow time for your wrist to settle: the default two-flick
gesture accepts a second flick within two seconds, up from 0.9 seconds.

There is also an optional **Light the screen, then flick once** mode in
**A window on the day → Panel gesture**. Wake the backlight normally, pause
briefly, then flick once to change the bottom panel. It listens for motion
only while the light is on and the watchface is visible, without continuous
accelerometer sampling or a timer to poll the light. Your existing gesture
choice is preserved.

The night saver's paused display now refreshes on any backlight wake, including
a screen touch or button. Pebble currently reserves touchscreen input for
watchapps, so screen taps themselves cannot change a watchface's panels.

Gesture bursts, settings changes, leaving the watchface and clock adjustments
are guarded against accidental or stuck changes. Physical wrist sensitivity
still depends on the watch's motion detection.
