# Dymaxion 0.4.8

Quiet Time now shows in the top bar: three small Zs beside the Bluetooth
rune while it is on.

The battery can show as a small battery. Turn on Battery gauge and the
percentage sits inside a battery outline, shaded as far as the charge. It is
off by default. While the watch charges, a bolt takes the place of the %
sign, and at or below the level where animations stop, the battery turns the
accent color.

A new Step line draws a thin line under the top bar that grows with today's
steps and reaches the edge at a typical day's total, from Pebble Health. It
is off by default.

The tide chart marks each high and low tide with a dotted line at its time,
and labels each high tide with its height, over the peak when there is room
and just under it when there is not. The tide header lists both the next
high and the next low, soonest first, each marked with a triangle: pointing
up for high water, down for low.

The weather chart labels the warmest and coolest readings, and a short grey
line marks each sunrise and sunset on the daylight strip. Its header lists
both the next sunrise and the next sunset.

The night saver is now one choice: off, during set hours, during Quiet Time,
or both. Quiet Time no longer needs a set of hours to go with it.
