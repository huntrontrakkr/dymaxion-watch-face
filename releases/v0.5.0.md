# Dymaxion 0.5.0

Place times can now sit between the clock and the map, where the Dymaxion
nameplate goes. Choose Between the clock and the map under Place times
position: each place gets one line of its map symbol, its short label and
its time in the large figures, spread evenly across the width. The
nameplate steps aside while the times are there.

A wireframe icosahedron can sit beside the clock: the twenty-sided solid
seen straight onto one face, the facing triangle in the middle and the
others around it. It fills the side of the screen that Chamfer and the
Pebble fonts leave open. Choose it on the left or right under Beside the
clock. With it on, the clock stays to one side, and place times beside the
clock take the icosahedron's place when they show.

The watch app is smaller: the Broad clock's tile map is stored far more
compactly, which leaves room for what comes next.
