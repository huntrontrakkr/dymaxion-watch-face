# Dymaxion 0.7.1

The color picker in the phone settings and the workshop now offers four
ways to arrange the watch's 64 colors:

- **Honeycomb**, Pebble's own layout, as before.
- **RGB cube**: four small grids, red across and green down, one for each
  level of blue.
- **Lightness**: the colors by CIELAB hue, from red round to purple, with
  the lightest at the top of each column and the greys on their own.
- **Chromaticity**: each color placed on the CIE 1931 diagram, inside the
  horseshoe of pure spectral colors, with the sRGB triangle and the watch
  screen's own, smaller triangle.

**As on the watch** still shows every color as the screen renders it, in
every arrangement; in Lightness and Chromaticity the colors move to where
the screen actually puts them. The picker remembers the arrangement you
chose.

The drawing grid for your own place glyph now has square cells, and the
picker's colors keep their shape on phones; the settings page's button
styles had been stretching both.
