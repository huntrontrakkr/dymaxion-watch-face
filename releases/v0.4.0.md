# A world of places, easier to make your own

- A redesigned phone settings page with a pixel-perfect watch preview, day/evening samples, palette swatches and clear, expandable sections.
- Search for a city, choose the right region, and fill its coordinates and time zone automatically. Suggested short labels remain editable. More than 50 saved cities are available offline.
- City search is also available in the web workshop. Your chosen glyphs, custom colors and saved settings carry forward.
- A warmer introduction to the Dymaxion map, Buckminster Fuller and Shoji Sadao.
- Automated release checks now build and test both the website and native watchface before publishing a version tag to Pebble and GitHub.

Open Dymaxion’s settings in the Pebble app after updating. Your existing watch layout and preferences are preserved. Search needs an internet connection; previews and saved cities work offline. Preview weather and device readings are examples.
