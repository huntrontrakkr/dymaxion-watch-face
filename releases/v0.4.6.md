# Dymaxion 0.4.6

Times on the map are easier to read. Each one now sits in its own small
clearing, so triangle edges, background dots and leader lines no longer run
through the figures, and the clearing stays put as the minutes change. A new
largest size joins Map time size in Display. When a label cannot fit at the
size you chose, it steps down one size at a time instead of disappearing.

The nameplate now shows under the Broad and Span clocks. Those clocks used to
paint over it; the nameplate is now drawn back on top, including on the
once-a-minute redraw.

Solarized Dark and Sandstone now show the calendar's weekday letters and the
charts' night shading and daylight strip, which were nearly invisible against
their backgrounds.

The stacked hours-over-minutes clock has been retired. Faces that used it
open with the regular clock. The "light the screen, then flick once" panel
gesture is gone too, because it could not work reliably once the light was on.
Faces that used it switch panels with two wrist flicks instead.

The watch preview on the settings page now places map times exactly as the
watch does, and the store description is rewritten in plain language.
