# Dymaxion 0.9.0

Add a gentle backlight tint to your favorite palette. In **Appearance →
Backlight**, choose **Use theme color** for warm, cool, mint or rose white
matched to the palette. Paper, Monochrome, Signal and High Visibility use
neutral white. The workshop offers the same controls under **Character**.

Each preset remembers its own custom backlight color. Custom palettes also
keep their tint, including when copied, exported or imported. Pick any full
24-bit RGB color or enter its exact `#RRGGBB` value under **Backlight hex**.
Custom colors are preserved exactly; dark or saturated choices can reduce
readability. Built-in defaults remain gentle. **Reset backlight color**
restores the preset tint, or white for a custom palette.

**Use watch setting** remains the default. Brightness, timeout and normal
wake behavior stay under the watch's control. The theme tint resumes after
a notification; leaving the face restores the system color. The screen
preview does not simulate the physical backlight.

The new controls are available in all 18 translated settings languages.
