# Dymaxion 0.4.4

Tide setup can now find the nearest NOAA station for you. Enable Tide or open
NOAA tides in settings: if no station is saved, Dymaxion suggests the nearest
station with hourly predictions within 150 km of your phone. Up to five nearby
choices show their distances, and selecting one fills its ID, short label and
local time zone automatically.

Existing stations stay selected. Use Find nearby NOAA stations to explore
alternatives, or keep using coastal presets and custom station details. Choose
the station that best fits your waterway, then Save to watch.

The lookup runs during phone configuration and reuses the shared low-power
location fix. It adds no watch-side polling or sensors. Without location access
or nearby NOAA coverage, manual selection remains available.
