# Dymaxion 0.5.1

The sun on the map is now placed with the Astronomical Almanac's solar
formula, good to about a hundredth of a degree. The previous approximation
drifted up to 0.4 degrees near the equinoxes. The seasons, the day-night line
and sunrise and sunset times were already right to well under a pixel, so the
face looks the same; it is simply more exact, and the watch now uses one sun
for both the map and the panels, which makes the app slightly smaller.
