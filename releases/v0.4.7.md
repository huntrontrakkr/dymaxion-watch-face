# Dymaxion 0.4.7

Custom palette colors now come from the watch's own 64 colors. The color
fields in the designer and on the phone settings page used to open the system
picker, which offers millions of colors the watch cannot show; the watch then
rounded your choice to its nearest color, sometimes a very different one. Each
color field now opens a hexagon of the 64 colors, laid out in the same order
as Pebble's own color picker. Arrow keys move around it, Enter chooses, and
Escape closes it.

The Pebble Time screen does not show colors the way a monitor does. The
picker has two views: Screen colors, the exact values, and As on the watch,
which shows each color as Pebble measured it on a Pebble Time screen in
sunlight. The same choice appears beside the watch preview in the designer
and on the phone settings page, so you can see a whole face roughly as it
will look on your wrist. The preview only changes how it is shown; the
colors sent to the watch are the same either way.

The watch-color sampling comes from Pebble's Clay configuration library
(MIT license), credited in NOTICE.
