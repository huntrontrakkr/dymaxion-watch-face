# Weather where you are

- Weather now defaults to your phone's current location and time zone, alongside the main clock. Temperature, rain, humidity and daylight use the same forecast location.
- City naming and weather share a cached, coarse location fix. Weather works even if city-name lookup is unavailable or your clock caption is entered manually. No continuous location tracking is added.
- If permission or connection fails, valid cached weather is marked OLD; another city's forecast is never silently substituted.
- Saved-city forecasts remain available as an optional override, with sunrise/sunset times in that city's time zone.

Existing preferences are preserved. After updating, open **A window on the day → Weather & humidity → Forecast location** and choose **Current location**, then save to the watch. Allow location access for the Pebble phone app.
