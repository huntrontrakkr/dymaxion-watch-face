# Dymaxion 0.8.0

The face speaks your language. Dates, the calendar, panel titles and messages
are drawn in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish,
Turkish, Indonesian, Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Simplified and Traditional
Chinese, Japanese or Korean, in the watch's own pixel lettering: accents on its
capitals, and Cyrillic, Greek and East Asian characters from the open-licensed
Fusion Pixel font. The settings page is translated into all of them, and into
Arabic (right to left) and Hindi, where the face itself stays in English for
now. It follows your phone's language unless you choose another under
**Language** at the top of the settings.

Tides now work beyond the United States. Where there is no NOAA station
within 150 km, the tide panel shows Open-Meteo's modelled sea level for where
you are, with its highs and lows. **Anywhere: modelled tides** in the station
list keeps it at any coastal place you choose. Modelled tides are good for the
shape of the day, not for navigation.

When a long Health heading would run into its right-hand label, it now drops
the heart rate rather than overlap.
