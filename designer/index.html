The Dymaxion map

The map projects the globe onto the twenty faces of an icosahedron and unfolds it without splitting a continent. Fuller worked on it for decades and called the result a deck plan of Spaceship Earth. It shows the planet as one island in one ocean, with no country at the center. He developed the final version with Shoji Sadao.

02

Buckminster Fuller

R. Buckminster Fuller (1895–1983) was an architect, designer and inventor, best known for the geodesic dome. Much of his work asked how to do more with less, a trend he called ephemeralization.
