Three places. Independent clocks. A shared planet.

Each place can have its own glyph and 64-color Pebble hue. Named time zones handle daylight saving time; coordinates place each marker. Beside a clock, +1 means tomorrow there, −1 means yesterday; no mark means the same calendar date as your watch.
