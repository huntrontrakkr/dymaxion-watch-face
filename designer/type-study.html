What to look for

Compare the space around the colon, the shape of 2 and 4, the openings in 6 and 9, and the rhythm of 11:11 against 08:08. Clock numerals have equal advances; letters keep their natural widths.

Oldstyle figures rise and fall like lowercase letters. Span has lining numerals only, because stable positions make the large time easier to read. Try the oldstyle option on the other studies. Recursive also has no oldstyle set.

The original nameplate script is drawn directly on a 124 × 17 pixel grid. Its wide, low lowercase and horizontal joins give the watch a mid-century identity; the information lettering carries the readouts.

These are bitmap proofs using the same FreeType monochrome loading flags as Pebble's SDK. Span and Draft have exact pixel masters, with FreeType round-trip checks for every glyph. The new clock cut takes substantial inspiration from Seth Haller's Dymaxion v.1 without using its outlines. Physical e-paper readability remains to be checked on a watch. The reference fonts retain their designers' authorship and SIL Open Font Licenses.
